POL-003: Remote, Hybrid, and International Work Policy

1. Purpose and Work Modes

Northstar Cloud Labs, Inc. operates a flexible Hybrid-First model centered around four regional hubs (New York, Austin, Toronto, and London), complemented by approved domestic Remote roles. Working across state or national borders triggers strict corporate tax nexus, permanent establishment, immigration, payroll withholding, and cybersecurity obligations. This policy governs domestic remote work, hybrid attendance, and temporary international remote work ("workcations").

2. Domestic Hybrid and Remote Eligibility

2.1 Hybrid Hub Attendance

Employees designated as Hybrid in the People Portal are expected to work on-site from their assigned regional hub at least 3 days per week (Tuesday through Thursday core collaboration days). Occasional remote weeks within the employee's home country require direct manager awareness.

2.2 Domestic Cross-State Relocation or Extended Remote Stays

Working remotely from a different US state, Canadian province, or UK region for more than 14 consecutive calendar days requires advance notice to People Operations so payroll tax withholding and workers' compensation registrations can be verified. Permanent relocation to another domestic state or country requires formal written compensation and tax review prior to moving.

2.3 Probationary Restriction

Employees within their initial 90-day onboarding and probation period (standing = Probation) are not eligible for extended out-of-state remote arrangements or temporary international remote work (see POL-007 Section 3.1).

3. Temporary International Remote Work ("Workcations")

Eligible Full-Time Regular Employees (FTEs) in Good Standing who have completed their 90-day probation period may request temporary international remote work subject to strict duration, jurisdiction, security, and approval rules.

3.1 Maximum 30-Calendar-Day Rolling Limit

An eligible employee may work remotely from an approved foreign country for a maximum of 30 calendar days total per rolling 12-month period (counted across all international work trips combined, inclusive of weekends falling within the remote stay). Any prior international remote days used within the past 12 months count toward the 30-calendar-day cap. For example, if an employee already used 5 international remote days earlier in the rolling 12-month window, only 25 remaining days are available under the standard cap.

Requests Exceeding 30 Calendar Days: Requests longer than 30 calendar days (for example, a 6-week / 42-day stay) are non-compliant with standard policy and cannot be self-approved by a manager. Such requests must either be shortened to fit within the remaining 30-day cap (taking PTO for additional days) or escalated for a formal Global Mobility, Tax, Legal, and VP Exception Review.

3.2 Approved vs. Restricted Jurisdictions

  • Pre-Approved Low-Tax-Nexus Countries (Up to 30 Days): Portugal, Spain, France, Germany, Netherlands, Ireland, Italy, United Kingdom, Canada, United States, Costa Rica, Mexico, Japan, and Australia—provided the employee already holds valid passport/visa entry rights allowing remote incidental business or holds EU/local work authorization. Northstar does not sponsor tourist-to-work visas for workcations.
  • Prohibited & Sanctions-Restricted Countries: Employees may never take company laptops or access Northstar networks from OFAC/UK/EU sanctioned regions or high-risk IP jurisdictions (including Russia, Belarus, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba, and mainland China without dedicated executive InfoSec clearance).

3.3 Mandatory Multi-Stakeholder Approval Workflow

Every temporary international remote work request must be submitted at least 15 business days prior to departure and receive documented sign-off from three stakeholders:

  1. Direct Manager: Confirms time-zone overlap (minimum 4 core overlap hours with the employee's home team) and project delivery continuity.
  2. People Operations & Global Mobility: Verifies remaining rolling 12-month day allowance (30-day cap), probation completion, and destination country eligibility.
  3. Information Security (InfoSec): Verifies employee security tier and travel device controls under POL-005.

Requests exceeding 30 rolling days or involving restricted jurisdictions additionally require Department VP and Legal/Tax Counsel sign-off.

4. Cybersecurity and Equipment Rules Abroad

Remote work outside an employee's home country is strictly conditioned on compliance with POL-005 (Information Security Policy):

  • Mandatory corporate WireGuard VPN and hardware FIDO2 MFA at all times.
  • Employees holding elevated security tiers (Prod-Admin or Customer-PII) must use an InfoSec-issued clean travel loaner laptop and cannot travel abroad with their primary production workstation.
  • Standard corporate tiers (Standard-Corp, Standard-Dev) may travel with their primary encrypted corporate laptop provided full-disk encryption and remote-wipe MDM are verified prior to departure.

5. Travel Expenses and Stipend Boundaries

Voluntary temporary international remote work ("workcations") is personal travel. Northstar does not reimburse personal airfare, lodging, meals, coworking passes, or international cell roaming incurred during voluntary remote stays. Business travel directed by the company is governed separately by POL-004.
