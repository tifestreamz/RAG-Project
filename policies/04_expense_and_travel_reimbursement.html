POL-004: Expense, Travel, and Home Office Reimbursement Policy

  • Document ID: POL-004
  • Policy Title: Expense, Travel, and Home Office Reimbursement Policy
  • Category: expenses
  • Effective Date: January 1, 2026
  • Owner: Northstar Finance & People Operations (people-ops@northstarcloud.example)

1. Purpose and General Reimbursement Principles

Northstar Cloud Labs, Inc. reimburses employees for reasonable, documented, and pre-authorized business expenses incurred while performing company duties. Employees are expected to exercise the same prudence when spending company funds as they would with personal funds. All expense claims must be submitted through the Expense Portal within 30 calendar days of the transaction date. Claims submitted after 60 days require VP Finance exception sign-off.

Itemized Receipt Requirement: Itemized merchant receipts showing line-item charges, date, vendor name, and tax are mandatory for any single expense exceeding $25.00 USD (or equivalent local currency). Credit card authorization slips that omit itemized food/beverage details are not accepted.

2. Business Travel: Airfare, Rail, and Ground Transport

  • Airfare Cabin Class: Domestic and short-haul flights under 6 hours single-leg scheduled duration must be booked in Economy / Main Cabin. Flights with a single scheduled leg of 6 hours or longer (such as transatlantic US-to-London or transpacific routes) qualify for Premium Economy. Business Class requires written CFO or CEO approval for documented medical accommodations or extraordinary client urgency.
  • Advance Booking & Ancillary Fees: Flights should be booked at least 14 calendar days in advance whenever possible. Standard seat selection fees and one checked bag for trips exceeding 3 nights are reimbursable. Airline lounge day passes, Wi-Fi subscriptions for personal use, TSA PreCheck/Global Entry renewals (except for travelers taking 4+ trips/year), and personal companion tickets are non-reimbursable.
  • Rail & Ground Transit: Standard-class rail (Amtrak Northeast Regional, Eurostar Standard, UK National Rail) is encouraged for regional trips under 4 hours. Economy rideshares (UberX/Lyft Standard), airport shuttles, and public transit are reimbursable with receipts.
  • Rental Cars & Mileage: Compact or mid-size rental vehicles are permitted when rideshare is impractical; employees must decline supplemental collision insurance on US domestic rentals as Northstar's corporate card provides primary coverage. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate.

3. Lodging and Hotel Caps

Standard hotel lodging for approved business travel is capped at $275.00 USD per night (before local occupancy taxes and mandatory fees). For designated High-Cost Tier Cities (New York City, San Francisco, Boston, London, Zurich, Tokyo, and Singapore), the nightly lodging cap increases to $350.00 USD per night.

If major conference demand pushes all reasonable properties above the nightly cap, the employee must attach screenshots showing comparable market pricing and obtain pre-trip written manager approval prior to booking. Personal hotel incidentals (in-room movies, minibar snacks, laundry for trips under 5 nights, gym fees, or spa services) are strictly non-reimbursable.

4. Daily Meals and Entertainment Limits

  • Individual Travel Meals: Capped at $75.00 USD per day for domestic travel and $100.00 USD per day for international travel (inclusive of tax and up to 20% gratuity). Unused daily meal allowances do not roll over to subsequent days.
  • Alcohol Boundary: Alcohol consumed as part of a business meal is limited to at most $25.00 USD within the daily meal cap. Standalone bar tabs without a meal are non-reimbursable.
  • Team Dinners & Client Meals: Capped at $85.00 USD per person (inclusive of tax and tip). The expense report must list every attendee's full name, company, and the business purpose of the meeting.

5. Home Office Ergonomic Stipend ($500 Limit)

Full-time regular employees (FTEs) and eligible part-time regular employees receive a $500.00 USD lifetime Home Office Ergonomic Stipend (refreshed every 4 years of continuous employment) to build a safe and ergonomic remote workspace.

5.1 Eligible Ergonomic Purchases

  • Ergonomic office chair, kneeling chair, or medical lumbar support cushion
  • Height-adjustable standing desk, motorized desk frame, or desktop standing converter
  • Single or dual external monitor arms, laptop risers, ergonomic split keyboards, or vertical mice
  • Dedicated anti-glare desk lamp or acoustic desk mat

5.2 Strict Exclusions and Contractor Ineligibility

1099 Independent Contractors and Fixed-Term Interns are strictly ineligible for the $500 Home Office Ergonomic Stipend (see POL-012 Section 2). Additionally, personal laptops, tablets, smartphones, ultrawide gaming monitors above $350, speakers, coffee machines, decorative rugs/artwork, and monthly home internet or utility bills are not reimbursable under the Home Office Stipend. All primary computing hardware must be procured directly through the IT Asset Portal under POL-008.

6. Non-Reimbursable Purchases, Conference Split Rules, and Audit Compliance

Self-Purchased Laptops Prohibited: Personal laptop purchases made out-of-pocket at retail stores without prior written CTO/IT exemption are never reimbursable via expense reports (POL-008 Section 3).

Conference & L&D Expense Splitting: When an employee attends an approved professional conference (POL-006 Section 6), conference registration tickets are charged against the employee's $2,500/year Learning & Development (L&D) budget, while compliant airfare, hotel lodging ($275/$350 cap), and daily meals ($75/$100 cap) are charged to the department travel budget—unless total combined costs remain within the employee's remaining L&D balance and receive manager + VP pre-approval.

Expense Audit & Policy Exceptions: Finance audits 100% of claims over $500 and a random 20% sample of all other reports. Repeated submission of out-of-policy expenses or split receipts designed to bypass the $25 receipt rule may result in corporate card suspension and disciplinary action under POL-010.
