# POL-006: Health, Retirement, and Wellness Benefits Policy

- **Document ID:** POL-006
- **Policy Title:** Health, Retirement, and Wellness Benefits Policy
- **Category:** `benefits`
- **Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
- **Owner:** Northstar Total Rewards & People Operations (`people-ops@northstarcloud.example`)

## 1. Purpose and Overview

Northstar Cloud Labs, Inc. provides a competitive, comprehensive benefits program supporting employees' physical health, financial retirement readiness, continuous professional growth, and everyday well-being. This document outlines core eligibility requirements, health insurance tiers, 401(k) / pension retirement matching, monthly wellness stipends, and annual Learning & Development (L&D) budgets.

## 3. Medical, Dental, and Vision Coverage

### 3.1 Enrollment Window
New full-time hires must complete benefits elections in the Benefits Portal within **30 calendar days** of their hire date. Coverage is effective retroactively to the first of the month coinciding with or following the start date (or Day 1 for hires starting on the 1st). Employees who miss the 30-day new-hire window must wait until Open Enrollment (November 1–15) unless they experience a Qualifying Life Event (QLE) such as marriage, birth/adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage.

### 3.2 Plan Tiers (US)
- **Platinum PPO:** Northstar pays **95%** of employee premiums and **80%** of dependent premiums. Low $250 deductible.
- **Gold PPO:** Northstar pays **90%** of employee premiums and **75%** of dependent premiums. $750 deductible.
- **Silver HDHP + HSA:** Northstar pays **100%** of employee-only premiums and contributes **$1,000/year** (single) or **$2,000/year** (family) directly into the employee's Health Savings Account (HSA).

Canada (Toronto Hub) and UK (London Hub) FTEs receive supplemental private medical, dental, vision, and prescription coverage tailored to provincial/NHS coordination.

## 4. Retirement Savings & 401(k) Match

### 4.1 US 401(k) Plan
US FTEs become eligible to enroll on the **1st day of the month following 30 days of continuous employment**.
- **Employer Match:** Northstar matches **100% of employee contributions up to 4.0% of eligible base salary**, per pay period.
- **Vesting Schedule:** Both employee deferrals and Northstar employer matching contributions are **100% immediately vested** upon deposit.
- **Investment Guidance Guardrail:** Neither Northstar People Operations nor internal AI HR tools may provide personalized stock picks, asset allocation recommendations, or tax/investment advice. Employees seeking financial advice should contact the plan's licensed fiduciary advisor via the Benefits Portal.

### 4.2 Canada RRSP & UK Workplace Pension
Toronto FTEs receive a matching **4.0% Group RRSP contribution**, and London FTEs receive an equivalent **4.0% Employer Pension Match** integrated with statutory auto-enrollment rules.

## 5. Wellness Stipend ($100/Month)

Every eligible FTE receives up to **$100.00 USD per month** (or local currency equivalent) in reimbursable wellness benefits.
- **Eligible Expenses:** Gym/fitness memberships, yoga/Pilates classes, running/cycling race registrations, meditation/mental-health apps, massage therapy, and ergonomic fitness trackers.
- **Ineligible Expenses:** Meal delivery services, personal clothing/sneakers (unless specialized athletic wear clearly tied to fitness memberships is excluded—footwear and general apparel are excluded), alcohol, or family-member memberships not covering the employee.
- **Non-Rollover Rule:** Unused monthly wellness amounts do **not** roll over from month to month. Claims must be submitted via the expense portal within 30 days of purchase (**POL-004**).

## 6. Learning & Development (L&D) Budget ($2,500/Year)

Northstar invests **$2,500.00 per calendar year** per FTE toward professional growth:
- **Eligible Expenses:** Technical or leadership conferences, accredited online courses, professional certifications (AWS, GCP, PMP, design workshops), and work-relevant technical books.
- **Pre-Approval Rule:** Any single L&D purchase or conference package exceeding **$500** requires **prior manager approval** before booking.
- **Interaction with Travel Caps:** When attending a conference in person, **conference registration fees** draw from the **$2,500 L&D budget**, whereas **airfare, hotel lodging, and daily meals** must follow standard travel reimbursement caps defined in **POL-004: Expense and Travel Reimbursement Policy** ($275/night standard hotel cap, $75/day domestic meal cap, economy flight <6 hrs). If combined conference registration + travel exceeds the employee's remaining L&D budget, the department manager and VP must approve any overage from departmental travel funds before booking.

## 7. Home Office Ergonomic Stipend ($500)

Eligible FTEs receive a **one-time $500.00 lifetime stipend** (refreshed every 4 years) for home office furniture and ergonomic accessories (office chair, standing desk converter, monitor arm, external keyboard/mouse). Full reimbursement conditions and itemized receipt rules are governed by **POL-004 Section 5**.
