# POL-010: Workplace Conduct, Ethics, and Anti-Harassment Policy

- **Document ID:** POL-010
- **Policy Title:** Workplace Conduct, Ethics, and Anti-Harassment Policy
- **Category:** `conduct`
- **Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
- **Owner:** Northstar People Operations & Legal (`ethics@northstarcloud.example`)

## 1. Core Principles of Workplace Conduct

Northstar Cloud Labs, Inc. is committed to providing a safe, respectful, inclusive, and ethical working environment for every employee, contractor, intern, customer, and partner. Every member of the Northstar community—regardless of seniority, title, hub location, or remote status—must uphold professional integrity, psychological safety, and zero tolerance for discrimination, harassment, bullying, or retaliation.

## 2. Anti-Harassment, Non-Discrimination, and Zero Retaliation

### 2.1 Protected Characteristics and Prohibited Conduct
Northstar strictly prohibits discrimination or harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, genetic information, or any other characteristic protected by applicable federal, state, provincial, or UK equality law. Prohibited conduct includes verbal slurs, demeaning jokes, unwelcome sexual advances, physical intimidation, hostile Slack/email communications, exclusionary conduct, and abusive behavior in physical offices or virtual meetings.

### 2.2 Strict Prohibition Against Retaliation
Northstar strictly prohibits **any form of retaliation** against an individual who in good faith reports suspected harassment, discrimination, safety hazards, financial misconduct, or policy violations, or who participates as a witness in an HR investigation. Examples of prohibited retaliation include:
- Sudden adverse project reassignments, removal from high-visibility teams, or exclusion from essential meetings after raising an HR concern.
- Unwarranted negative performance reviews, sudden Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs), or denial of earned promotion/PTO requests immediately following a protected complaint.
- Subtle intimidation, social ostracizing, or disclosing confidential complaint details to unauthorized peers.

## 3. Mandatory Human Escalation Rule for Sensitive HR Cases

### 3.1 When Automated Systems and Managers Must Escalate Immediately
Sensitive interpersonal, legal, or workplace safety matters **cannot be resolved solely through automated self-service guidance**. Whenever an employee raises an issue involving any of the following triggers, HR assistants and managers must **immediately recommend confidential escalation** to People Operations / Ethics & Legal (`ethics@northstarcloud.example`) and prepare a confidential **Priority P1** HR case summary (**POL-011**):
- Allegations of workplace harassment, discrimination, bullying, or hostile work environment.
- Suspected **manager retaliation**, abuse of authority, or unfair adverse action after raising a concern.
- Physical safety threats, self-harm risk, or workplace violence.
- Whistleblower reports involving financial fraud, bribery, insider trading, falsified expense/time records, or customer data breaches.
- Requests for disability/medical accommodations or sensitive leave disputes involving potential legal exposure.

### 3.2 Confidentiality, Investigation Protocol, and Non-Irreversible Triage
Reports are treated on a strict need-to-know basis by trained People Operations investigators and outside employment counsel where appropriate. Automated HR tools must **never** notify an accused manager directly, send unconfirmed emails about sensitive personnel complaints, or modify employee records without explicit user confirmation to submit an encrypted People Ops / Ethics case ticket.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest, Gifts, and Outside Employment

- **Business Gifts & Hospitality Cap:** Employees may not solicit or accept gifts, meals, entertainment, conference swag, or favors from vendors, customers, or partners valued above **$50.00 USD** per occasion (or **$100.00 USD** cumulative per vendor per calendar year). Cash or cash-equivalent gift cards are strictly prohibited regardless of value.
- **Conflicts of Interest:** Employees must disclose any financial interest in, board advisory role with, or close family/romantic relationship with a vendor, competitor, customer, or direct reporting line to People Operations and Legal within 10 business days of discovery.
- **Outside Employment:** Moonlighting, freelance consulting, or secondary employment for a direct competitor or conducted during Northstar core working hours is prohibited. Open-source software contributions and academic writing unrelated to proprietary Northstar IP are permitted provided they comply with the Employee Invention Assignment Agreement and open-source review policy.
- **Acceptable Political & Social Discourse:** Respectful dialogue is welcomed in designated community channels, while customer-facing channels and engineering forums must remain strictly professional and inclusive.

## 5. Disciplinary Framework and Good-Faith Protections

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action up to and including written warnings, loss of bonus eligibility, revocation of remote work privileges, or immediate termination of employment or contractor engagement. Knowingly submitting a maliciously fabricated accusation is itself a violation of workplace integrity; however, any report made in honest good faith is fully protected even if an investigation ultimately proves inconclusive.
