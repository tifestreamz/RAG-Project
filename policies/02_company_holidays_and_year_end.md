# POL-002: Company Holidays and Year-End Shutdown Policy

- **Document ID:** POL-002
- **Policy Title:** Company Holidays and Year-End Shutdown Policy
- **Category:** `pto_holidays`
- **Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
- **Owner:** Northstar People Operations (`people-ops@northstarcloud.example`)

## 1. Purpose

Northstar Cloud Labs, Inc. observes paid statutory and cultural holidays across our operating hubs in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom, alongside an annual company-wide Year-End Winter Recharge shutdown. This policy explains observed holiday calendars, floating holiday entitlements, weekend observation rules, and how company closures interact with personal PTO requests under **POL-001**.

## 2. Eligibility

All **Full-Time Regular Employees (FTEs)** and pro-rated Part-Time Regular Employees (>=20 hrs/week) receive paid company holidays and floating holidays beginning on Day 1 of employment (no 90-day waiting period applies to scheduled company holidays). **1099 Contractors and Fixed-Term Interns** are not paid for unworked company holidays unless explicitly stated in their statement of work (see **POL-012**).

## 3. Annual Paid Company Holiday Calendar (2026)

Northstar observes **11 core paid holidays** in the United States (with equivalent regional statutory schedules in Canada and the UK). In 2026, the US core schedule includes:

1. **New Year's Day:** Thursday, January 1, 2026
2. **Martin Luther King Jr. Day:** Monday, January 19, 2026
3. **Presidents' Day:** Monday, February 16, 2026
4. **Memorial Day:** Monday, May 25, 2026
5. **Juneteenth National Independence Day:** Friday, June 19, 2026
6. **Independence Day (Observed):** Friday, July 3, 2026 (July 4 falls on Saturday)
7. **Labor Day:** Monday, September 7, 2026
8. **Indigenous Peoples' Day / Columbus Day:** Monday, October 12, 2026
9. **Thanksgiving Day:** Thursday, November 26, 2026
10. **Day After Thanksgiving:** Friday, November 27, 2026
11. **Christmas Eve & Christmas Day Observation:** Thursday, December 24, 2026 and Friday, December 25, 2026 (see Section 4 below)

### 3.1 Regional Hub Substitutions (Canada & UK)
- **Toronto Hub (CA):** Replaces US federal holidays with Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day (July 1), Civic Holiday, Labour Day, National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, Thanksgiving (October), and Boxing Day (December 26 observed).
- **London Hub (UK):** Observes New Year's Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Early May Bank Holiday, Spring Bank Holiday, Summer Bank Holiday, Christmas Day, and Boxing Day.

### 3.2 Weekend Observation Rule
When a fixed-date company holiday falls on a **Saturday**, Northstar observes the paid holiday on the preceding **Friday**. When a fixed-date holiday falls on a **Sunday**, Northstar observes the paid holiday on the following **Monday**.

## 4. Annual Winter Recharge Shutdown (December 26 – December 31)

To allow our teams to disconnect simultaneously without returning to an overwhelming backlog, Northstar closes corporate operations globally from **December 26 through December 31** every year ("Winter Recharge").

### 4.1 Zero PTO Deduction Rule
All working weekdays falling within **December 24 – December 25** (Christmas Eve and Christmas Day company holidays) and **December 26 – December 31** (Winter Recharge Shutdown) are **100% company-paid days off** and **never deduct from an employee's accrued PTO balance**.

### 4.2 Calculating PTO Around the Winter Shutdown
When an employee requests time off adjacent to the Winter Recharge shutdown (for example, the week of **December 21 – December 25, 2026**):
- **Monday, Dec 21:** Regular workday — requires **1.0 PTO day** (or floating holiday).
- **Tuesday, Dec 22:** Regular workday — requires **1.0 PTO day**.
- **Wednesday, Dec 23:** Regular workday — requires **1.0 PTO day**.
- **Thursday, Dec 24 (Christmas Eve Observed):** Company Paid Holiday — **0.0 PTO days deducted**.
- **Friday, Dec 25 (Christmas Day):** Company Paid Holiday — **0.0 PTO days deducted**.
- **Monday, Dec 28 – Thursday, Dec 31 (Winter Recharge):** Company Paid Shutdown — **0.0 PTO days deducted**.

Accordingly, an employee taking the full 5-business-day week of **December 21 – December 25** off only uses **3.0 PTO days** (Dec 21, 22, 23), while remaining off continuously through January 1, 2027 (observing New Year's Day). Notice and manager approval rules under **POL-001 Section 4** still apply based on the length of the requested PTO block (3 consecutive PTO days requires **10 business days advance notice** and **manager approval**).

### 4.3 Essential On-Call Exceptions During Shutdown
Infrastructure SREs or Customer Escalation engineers scheduled for critical P1 coverage during Winter Recharge receive **1.5x compensatory floating PTO days** for every shutdown day worked, which must be scheduled with their manager within Q1 of the following year.

## 5. Floating Holidays

In addition to the scheduled calendar, every eligible FTE receives **2 paid Floating Holidays** each calendar year (granted on January 1; new hires starting after July 1 receive 1 floating holiday for the remainder of the year).
- Floating holidays may be used for religious observances, cultural celebrations, birthdays, or personal recharge days.
- Floating holidays must be taken in full-day increments, require **3 business days advance notice** to the direct manager, and **expire on December 31** with zero carryover or payout.
- Floating holidays may be combined with regular PTO adjacent to Winter Recharge (e.g., applying 1 floating holiday + 2 PTO days for December 21–23).
