# POL-007: Onboarding and Probationary Period Policy

- **Document ID:** POL-007
- **Policy Title:** Onboarding and Probationary Period Policy
- **Category:** `onboarding`
- **Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
- **Owner:** Northstar People Operations (`people-ops@northstarcloud.example`)

## 1. Purpose

Northstar Cloud Labs, Inc. operates a structured **90-calendar-day onboarding and probationary period** for all new full-time and part-time regular employees. This policy defines first-week compliance milestones, equipment provisioning, 30/60/90-day check-in expectations, and temporary benefit/leave limitations during the probation window.

## 2. Onboarding Timeline and Mandatory Milestones

### 2.1 Day 1 – Week 1 Checklist
Every new hire must complete the following mandatory setup items within their first **5 business days**:
1. **Identity & Payroll Setup:** Verify I-9 / right-to-work documentation, direct deposit, and tax withholding forms in the People Portal.
2. **Hardware & Security Provisioning:** Activate corporate laptop with full-disk encryption (FileVault or BitLocker), register at least two hardware FIDO2 MFA keys (YubiKey), and configure the corporate WireGuard VPN client (**POL-005**).
3. **Mandatory Compliance Trainings:** Complete *Information Security & Data Protection 101*, *Anti-Harassment & Inclusive Workplace Conduct* (**POL-010**), and *Expense Policy Essentials* (**POL-004**) within **10 business days** of start date.

### 2.2 30 / 60 / 90-Day Manager Check-Ins
Managers must conduct and document structured milestone reviews at **Day 30** (role clarity & tool readiness), **Day 60** (initial project delivery & team collaboration), and **Day 90** (probation completion sign-off). Successful completion of Day 90 transitions the employee's status from `Probation` to `Good Standing`.

## 3. Policies and Restrictions During the 90-Day Probation Period

New hires receive Day-1 eligibility for company holidays (**POL-002**), medical insurance (1st of month / Day 1 under **POL-006**), and IT-issued standard laptops (**POL-008**). However, the following probationary guardrails apply during the first **90 calendar days**:

### 3.1 Remote Work and International Workcations
- New hires whose offer letter specifies an **On-site** or **Hybrid** anchor hub must work from their assigned hub during the 90-day probation period unless an accommodation is approved by People Operations.
- **International Remote Work ("Workcations")** under **POL-003** are **strictly prohibited** during the first 90 days of employment. Eligibility for temporary international remote work begins only after completing probation (`tenure_days >= 90` and `standing == "Good Standing"`).

### 3.2 PTO Usage Limits During Probation
- New hires accrue PTO from Day 1 (0.625 days per pay period in the 0–2 year tier), meaning an employee at Day 55 has typically accrued ~2.5 PTO days.
- Probationary employees may **not** borrow PTO into a negative balance (**POL-001 Section 3.1**).
- Requests for **3 or more consecutive PTO days** during the 90-day probation period are restricted unless pre-disclosed prior to signing the offer letter or approved as a documented emergency exception by the **manager and People Operations**.

### 3.3 Parental Leave and L&D Budget
- **Parental Leave (16 weeks paid)** requires **6 months (180 days) of continuous tenure** at the time of birth or placement (**POL-009**). Employees with fewer than 180 days of tenure are ineligible for full paid parental leave, though statutory unpaid/short-term leave options remain coordinated with People Operations.
- **L&D Budget ($2,500/yr)** and the **$500 Home Office Ergonomic Stipend** are available to FTEs after Week 1 onboarding completion, subject to manager pre-approval for L&D expenses over $500 (**POL-006**, **POL-004**).

## 4. Extending or Failing Probation

If performance or conduct concerns arise before Day 90, the manager must consult People Operations no later than Day 65 to establish a written 30-day coaching plan or extend probation by up to 30 additional days. Separation during probation remains subject to applicable local employment laws.
