# POL-001: Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Request Policy

- **Document ID:** POL-001
- **Policy Title:** Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Request Policy
- **Category:** `pto_holidays`
- **Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
- **Owner:** Northstar People Operations (`people-ops@northstarcloud.example`)

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Northstar Cloud Labs, Inc. ("Northstar") believes that intentional rest, personal travel, and time away from work are essential to sustained creativity, well-being, and high performance. This Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy establishes accrual rates, eligibility rules, carryover limits, advance notice thresholds, approval workflows, and blackout period rules for eligible employees across our United States, Canada, and United Kingdom hubs.

This policy governs general vacation, personal days, and routine wellness time off. Separate provisions apply to statutory company holidays and the annual Winter Recharge shutdown (see **POL-002: Company Holidays and Year-End Shutdown Policy**) as well as parental, medical, FMLA, and bereavement leave (see **POL-009: Parental, Medical, and Bereavement Leave Policy**).

## 2. Eligibility and Employment Classification

### 2.1 Eligible Employees
Full-Time Regular Employees (FTEs) scheduled to work 30 or more hours per week are eligible to accrue and use paid PTO beginning on their hire date, subject to probationary scheduling guidelines during their first 90 days (see **POL-007: Onboarding and Probationary Period Policy**).

### 2.2 Ineligible Classifications
1099 Independent Contractors, Fixed-Term Interns, and part-time temporary workers scheduled for fewer than 20 hours per week do **not** accrue paid PTO and are not eligible for paid company holidays or floating holidays (see **POL-012: Contractor and Intern Guidelines**). Part-time regular employees working 20–29 hours per week accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis proportional to scheduled weekly hours.

## 3. Annual PTO Accrual Tiers

Northstar uses a tenure-based progressive accrual schedule over the January 1 – December 31 leave year. PTO accrues semi-monthly across 24 pay periods. Tenure tier upgrades take effect on the first day of the pay period immediately following the employee's work anniversary.

| Continuous Tenure | Annual PTO Allowance | Semi-Monthly Accrual | Max Rollover Cap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0 to < 2 years | 15 business days (120 hours) | 0.625 days / pay period | 5 business days |
| 2 to < 5 years | 20 business days (160 hours) | 0.833 days / pay period | 5 business days |
| 5+ years | 25 business days (200 hours) | 1.042 days / pay period | 5 business days |

### 3.1 Negative PTO Balances
Employees in good standing who have completed their 90-day probation period may borrow up to **2.0 days** of unaccrued PTO within the current calendar year only with written manager and People Operations approval. Probationary employees (first 90 days) may not carry a negative PTO balance.

### 3.2 Year-End Carryover and Forfeiture
Up to **5.0 unused PTO days** automatically carry over from one calendar year to the next on January 1. Unless local statutory law (such as California or Ontario vacation-pay statutes) prohibits forfeiture, any unused PTO above 5.0 days on December 31 is forfeited ("use it or lose it"). Northstar encourages employees to plan Q4 leave early so balances are not lost.

## 4. Advance Notice and Approval Requirements

To maintain operational continuity and fair coverage across teams, employees must submit PTO requests in the People Portal according to the consecutive business-day duration of the absence:

- **1 to 2 Consecutive Business Days:** Minimum **5 business days** advance notice required. Approved by direct manager. Emergency or acute illness time off does not require advance notice and should be logged as soon as practicable.
- **3 to 9 Consecutive Business Days:** Minimum **10 business days (2 weeks)** advance notice required. Requires direct **manager approval** and a documented handoff plan designating a backup point of contact.
- **10 or More Consecutive Business Days:** Minimum **20 business days (4 weeks)** advance notice required. Requires **both direct manager approval and Department VP approval** (or People Operations review if the VP is unavailable).

### 4.1 Interaction with Company Holidays and Winter Recharge
Company-observed holidays and the **Annual Winter Recharge Shutdown (December 26 – December 31)** are paid company-wide closures and do **not** consume an employee's personal PTO balance (see **POL-002 Section 4**). When an employee submits a multi-day PTO request spanning December 21 through December 25 or December 31, only regular working business days that are not company holidays or Winter Recharge days are deducted from the employee's PTO balance. For example, in a standard year where December 24 (Christmas Eve half/full observed holiday per POL-002) and December 25 (Christmas Day) are company holidays, taking off December 21–25 deducts only the non-holiday working days (typically 3.0 PTO days rather than 5.0).

## 5. Blackout Windows and Critical On-Call Coverage

### 5.1 Operational Blackout Periods
Infrastructure, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), and Enterprise Support teams designate quarterly release freezes and peak customer migration windows (typically the final 5 business days of Q4 or major product launch weeks) as **Managed Coverage Windows**. Employees flagged with `blackout_team_flag = true` in the HR system are not strictly banned from emergency leave, but discretionary PTO requests exceeding 2 consecutive days during a blackout window require VP approval and verified primary/secondary on-call swap confirmation.

### 5.2 Probationary Restrictions
During the initial **90-day onboarding and probation period**, new hires may take pre-negotiated leave disclosed prior to their start date or up to **2 consecutive PTO days** for urgent personal matters. Requests for 3 or more consecutive PTO days during probation require special People Operations and manager exception approval (see **POL-007 Section 3.2**).

## 6. Sick Time, Wellness Days, and Offboarding Payout

### 6.1 Paid Sick and Mental Health Days
Separate from vacation PTO, FTEs receive **8 paid Sick and Mental Health days** per calendar year (front-loaded on January 1, non-rollover). Sick days may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, caregiving for an immediate family member, or acute mental health recovery. Doctor's notes are not required for absences of 3 or fewer consecutive days.

### 6.2 Separation and Unused PTO
Upon voluntary resignation or involuntary separation, accrued and unused PTO is paid out in the final paycheck only where required by applicable state or provincial employment law (including California, Colorado, Illinois, Massachusetts, Canada statutory minimum vacation, and UK statutory holiday entitlement). Unused sick days and floating holidays are never paid out upon separation.

## 7. Policy Compliance and Exceptions

Submitting falsified leave records or taking unapproved extended absences without communication is a violation of workplace standards. Requests for policy exceptions (such as borrowing more than 2 days or exceeding 10 consecutive days during a blackout window) must be escalated to People Operations via an HR Operations ticket (`POL-011`).
